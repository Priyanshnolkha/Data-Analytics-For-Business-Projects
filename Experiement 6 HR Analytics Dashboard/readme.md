# 📊 HR Employee Attrition Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an **interactive HR Employee Attrition Dashboard** built using **Microsoft Power BI** based on the **IBM HR Analytics Employee Attrition Dataset**. The dashboard helps HR professionals analyze employee demographics, workforce distribution, attrition trends, job satisfaction, and employee performance to support data-driven workforce planning.

This project was developed as part of the **Data Analytics for Business - Weekly Assignment 6** at **Alliance University**.

---

## 🎯 Objective

The objective of this dashboard is to:

* Monitor employee attrition and workforce distribution
* Analyze employee demographics and job roles
* Track department-wise attrition trends
* Evaluate employee satisfaction and performance
* Support workforce planning and retention strategies

---

## 📊 Key Insights

* The organization has **1.47K employees** with **237 attritions**, resulting in an **Attrition Rate of 16.12%**.
* Research & Development has the highest employee count and attrition.
* Employees aged **26–35 years** show the highest attrition.
* Sales Executives, Research Scientists, and Laboratory Technicians experience higher employee turnover.
* Employees who work overtime or travel frequently show relatively higher attrition.
